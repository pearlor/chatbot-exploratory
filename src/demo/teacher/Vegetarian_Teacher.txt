## Creamy Tuscan Garlic Gnocchi

### [Ingredients] Chef Kale's Fresh Tuscan Selection
* **1 package (16 oz) potato gnocchi** (look for high-quality or fresh shelf-stable gnocchi)
* **1 tablespoon olive oil**
* **2 tablespoons unsalted butter**
* **4 cloves garlic**, finely minced
* **1 cup cherry tomatoes**, halved
* **2 cups fresh baby spinach**, packed
* **1/2 cup vegetable broth**
* **3/4 cup heavy cream** (or coconut cream for a dairy-free alternative)
* **1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese** (ensure it uses vegetarian-friendly microbial rennet)
* **1/2 teaspoon Italian seasoning**
* **Salt and freshly cracked black pepper** to taste
* **A pinch of red pepper flakes** (optional, for a touch of warmth)

### [Steps] Master the Tuscan Pan-Sear
1. **Boil the Gnocchi:** Bring a large pot of salted water to a rolling boil. Add the potato gnocchi and cook according to the package instructions (usually they only need 2 to 3 minutes, and they will float to the top when they are done). Drain them immediately and set them aside.
2. **Sauté the Aromatics:** In a large skillet, melt the butter with the olive oil over medium heat. Add the minced garlic and sauté for about 1 minute until it is fragrant and golden, being careful not to let it burn.
3. **Blister the Tomatoes:** Add the halved cherry tomatoes and Italian seasoning to the skillet. Cook for about 3 to 4 minutes until the tomatoes begin to soften and blister slightly, releasing their sweet juices.
4. **Build the Cream Sauce:** Pour in the vegetable broth and heavy cream. Bring the mixture to a gentle simmer, then reduce the heat to low and let it bubble gently for about 3 minutes to thicken slightly.
5. **Wilt and Melt:** Stir in the fresh baby spinach and grated Parmesan cheese. Stir continuously until the spinach is beautiful and wilted, and the cheese has fully melted into a velvety, smooth sauce. Season with salt, pepper, and optional red pepper flakes.
6. **Combine and Serve:** Add the cooked gnocchi directly into the skillet. Toss gently to coat every pillowy piece of gnocchi in that luscious cream sauce. Let it simmer together for 1 minute so the gnocchi absorbs the flavors. Serve hot with an extra sprinkle of Parmesan if you like!

### [Pros/Cons] Chef's Honest Assessment
* + Incredibly fast to make, going from pantry to plate in under 30 minutes.
* + A luxurious, restaurant-quality meal that uses simple, easy-to-find ingredients.
* + Highly customizable; you can easily add mushrooms, artichoke hearts, or white beans for extra protein.
* - The heavy cream makes this a richer, higher-calorie dish, so it may not fit lighter dietary plans.
* - Gnocchi can become mushy if overcooked in the water or left in the sauce too long before serving.
