**Time:** 30 minutes  
**Difficulty:** Easy  
**Serves:** 3-4  

Hello there, fellow chef! Chef Kale here. I looked into your fridge and pantry, and I have the perfect, delightful surprise for you. We are going to turn those fresh eggs, oranges, blueberries, and flour into a spectacular, puffy showstopper: a **Citrus-Infused Blueberry Dutch Baby**! 

A Dutch Baby is a giant, puffed pancake that rises dramatically in the oven. It has a custard-like center and crispy, tall edges. It is incredibly easy but looks like it came straight out of a high-end brunch spot. 

Here is what you need and how to make it:

## Citrus-Infused Blueberry Dutch Baby

### [Ingredients] Chef Kale's Pantry & Shopping List

**From your fridge and pantry:**
* **Eggs** (3 large, at room temperature)
* **Milk** (1/2 cup, preferably whole or 2%, at room temperature)
* **Flour** (1/2 cup all-purpose flour)
* **Sugar** (2 tablespoons, plus an extra teaspoon for the topping)
* **Blueberries** (1/2 cup)
* **Oranges** (1, for zest and juice)

**Ingredients you need to buy:**
* **Unsalted Butter** (3 tablespoons)
* *Optional:* **Powdered sugar** for dusting

---

### [Steps] How to Bake This Golden Wonder

1. **Preheat and Prep:** Place a 10-inch cast-iron skillet (or any heavy, oven-safe metal skillet) on the middle rack of your oven and preheat to **425°F (220°C)**.
2. **Blend the Batter:** In a blender, combine the **eggs**, **milk**, **flour**, **sugar**, and the finely grated zest of half an **orange**. Blend on high for 30 seconds until the mixture is completely smooth, frothy, and aerated. Let the batter rest in the blender for 10 minutes while the oven finishes heating.
3. **Melt the Butter:** Once the oven is hot, carefully remove the heated skillet using a thick oven mitt. Drop the **unsalted butter** into the hot pan and swirl it around until it is fully melted and bubbling.
4. **Assemble and Bake:** Scatter about half of your **blueberries** into the melted butter, then immediately pour the batter right into the center of the skillet. Quickly place the skillet back into the oven.
5. **Bake to Perfection:** Bake for **15 to 20 minutes** until the pancake is spectacularly puffed up, tall on the sides, and a deep golden brown. *Chef's tip: Do not open the oven door during baking, or the steam will escape and the pancake will collapse early!*
6. **Prepare the Topping:** While the Dutch Baby is baking, toss your remaining **blueberries** with 1 tablespoon of fresh **orange juice** and 1 teaspoon of **sugar** in a small bowl. 
7. **Serve Immediately:** Remove the pancake from the oven. It will start to deflate slightly as it cools—this is perfectly normal and creates a gorgeous well in the center! Spoon the fresh orange-macerated blueberries over the top, dust with powdered sugar if you have it, cut into wedges, and serve immediately.

---

### [Pros & Cons] Why You'll Love This (And What to Keep in Mind)

* + **Incredibly dramatic and beautiful presentation** with very minimal prep work.
* + **Uses simple pantry staples** that you already have in abundance in your kitchen.
* + **The combination of orange zest and warm blueberries** creates a bright, restaurant-quality flavor profile.
* - **It deflates quickly** after leaving the oven, so your guests need to be at the table ready to eat!
* - **Requires an oven-safe skillet**, preferably cast-iron, to achieve that signature dramatic rise.

### [Ending comment] Chef Kale's Final Kitchen Wisdom

You are going to do an amazing job with this recipe! Remember, the secret to a towering Dutch Baby is ensuring your eggs and milk are at room temperature before blending. This helps the batter trap steam and rise to its full potential. I can't wait for you to smell the sweet citrus and vanilla-like aroma filling your kitchen. Happy cooking, and bon appétit!
