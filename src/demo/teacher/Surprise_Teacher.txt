**Time:** 35 minutes  
**Difficulty:** Easy  
**Serves:** 4  

## Chef Kale's Crispy Gochujang Honey Brussels Sprouts

Hello there, aspiring chef! I am **Chef Kale**, and today I want to surprise you with a dish that has turned many vegetable skeptics into absolute believers. We are making **Crispy Gochujang Honey Brussels Sprouts**. 

This recipe takes the humble Brussels sprout and elevates it with a sweet, savory, and slightly spicy Korean-inspired glaze. It is incredibly easy to make, but the flavor profile is so complex and satisfying that it will feel like it came straight out of a professional kitchen. Let's get cooking!

### [Ingredients] What You'll Need for the Magic

*   **1 pound** Brussels sprouts, trimmed and halved lengthwise
*   **2 tablespoons** olive oil
*   **1/2 teaspoon** kosher salt
*   **1/4 teaspoon** freshly cracked black pepper
*   **2 tablespoons** Gochujang (Korean chili paste)
*   **1.5 tablespoons** honey (or maple syrup)
*   **1 tablespoon** low-sodium soy sauce
*   **1 teaspoon** rice vinegar
*   **1 teaspoon** toasted sesame oil
*   **1 tablespoon** toasted sesame seeds (for garnish)
*   **2 whole** green onions, thinly sliced (for garnish)

---

### [Steps] Let's Cook Together!

1.  **Prep the oven and pan:** Preheat your oven to **425°F (218°C)**. Place a large rimmed baking sheet inside the oven while it preheats. Heating the pan beforehand is my favorite professional secret for getting that ultra-crispy texture!
2.  **Season the sprouts:** In a large bowl, toss the halved Brussels sprouts with the **olive oil**, **kosher salt**, and **black pepper** until they are evenly coated.
3.  **Roast to perfection:** Carefully remove the hot baking sheet from the oven. Arrange the sprouts in a single layer, **cut-side down**. This maximizes contact with the hot metal, giving you a gorgeous, caramelized char. Roast for **20 to 25 minutes**, until they are tender and the outer leaves are crispy and dark brown.
4.  **Whisk the glaze:** While the sprouts are roasting, whisk together the **Gochujang**, **honey**, **soy sauce**, **rice vinegar**, and **toasted sesame oil** in a small bowl until smooth. 
5.  **Toss and coat:** Transfer the hot, roasted Brussels sprouts back into your mixing bowl. Pour the glaze over them and toss gently until every sprout is beautifully coated in the glossy sauce.
6.  **Garnish and serve:** Transfer to a serving platter. Garnish generously with the **toasted sesame seeds** and **sliced green onions**. Serve immediately while hot!

---

### Why You'll Love This (And a Few Things to Consider)

* + **Incredible Flavor Fusion:** The combination of sweet honey and spicy, fermented Gochujang creates an addictive umami flavor.
* + **Texture Heaven:** Roasting them cut-side down on a preheated pan guarantees a crispy exterior and tender interior.
* + **Skeptic Approved:** This is the ultimate recipe to win over anyone who thinks they do not like Brussels sprouts.
* - **Mild Heat Level:** Gochujang is a fermented chili paste, so it does carry a mild kick that might be too much for toddlers or very spice-sensitive palates.
* - **Ingredient Search:** Gochujang is a specialty ingredient, so you may need to look in the international aisle of your local grocery store or visit an Asian market.

### [Ending comment] Chef Kale's Kitchen Wisdom

Remember, cooking is all about confidence and experimentation! If you find the glaze a bit too spicy, feel free to add an extra splash of **honey** to sweeten it up. If you want more acidity, a squeeze of fresh **lime juice** at the very end works wonders. I am so proud of you for trying something new today. Keep practicing, keep tasting, and most importantly, have fun in your kitchen! Happy cooking!
