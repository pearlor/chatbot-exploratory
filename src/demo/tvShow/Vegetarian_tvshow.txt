## Creamy Tuscan Garlic Tortellini

### [Ingredients] Rosemary’s Fresh & Flavorful Basket
* **9 ounces (250g)** refrigerated cheese tortellini
* **1 tablespoon** olive oil
* **3 cloves** garlic, minced (bring on the aroma!)
* **1/2 cup** sun-dried tomatoes (packed in oil, drained and chopped)
* **2 cups** fresh baby spinach
* **1 cup** heavy cream (for that luxurious texture!)
* **1/2 cup** vegetable broth
* **1/2 cup** freshly grated Parmesan cheese
* **1 teaspoon** Italian seasoning
* **To taste** salt and freshly cracked black pepper

---

### [Steps] The Magic in the Skillet
1. **Boil the Pasta:** Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Drop in your **cheese tortellini** and cook according to the package instructions (usually just 3 to 4 minutes!). Drain them and set them aside.
2. **Sauté the Aromatics:** Heat your **olive oil** in a large skillet over medium heat. Toss in the **minced garlic** and **sun-dried tomatoes**. Sauté these beauties for about 1 minute until your kitchen smells like a rustic Italian villa!
3. **Build the Sauce:** Pour in the **vegetable broth**, **heavy cream**, and **Italian seasoning**. Bring it to a gentle, bubbling simmer and let those flavors dance together for about 3 minutes.
4. **Wilt the Greens:** Stir in your gorgeous **fresh baby spinach**. Watch it wilt right into that creamy sauce—this takes about 1 to 2 minutes. Beautiful colors!
5. **Cheesy Goodness:** Reduce the heat to low and stir in the **grated Parmesan cheese** until it melts into a smooth, silky dream. Season with a pinch of **salt and black pepper**.
6. **The Grand Finale:** Toss your cooked **tortellini** right into the skillet. Gently stir to coat every single pasta pocket in that glorious sauce. Serve it hot, and get ready for the compliments!

---

### Why This Dish is a Total Showstopper

* + Super quick to make, taking under 20 minutes from start to finish!
* + Incredibly rich and comforting flavor that even the most dedicated meat-lovers will adore.
* + Highly customizable—feel free to toss in sliced mushrooms or juicy cherry tomatoes!
* - This dish is wonderfully rich, so you may want to pair it with a light, crisp side salad to balance the meal.
* - Leftovers can soak up the sauce in the fridge, but a splash of milk or broth when reheating brings it right back to life!
