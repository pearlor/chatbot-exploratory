## Heavenly 3-Ingredient Peanut Butter Cookies

### [Ingredients] My Magical Three-Ingredient Lineup

*   **1 cup creamy peanut butter** (smooth and luscious is best here, viewers!)
*   **1 cup granulated sugar** 
*   **1 large egg**

### [Steps] The Simple Steps to Cookie Perfection

1.  **Preheat and Prep:** First things first, let's get our ovens nice and cozy! Preheat your oven to **350°F (175°C)** and line a baking sheet with some parchment paper. 
2.  **Mix with Love:** In a medium bowl, combine your **creamy peanut butter**, **granulated sugar**, and that **one beautiful egg**. Stir it all together until it forms a smooth, thick dough. It takes less than a minute, viewers! Look at how beautifully that comes together!
3.  **Roll and Shape:** Roll the dough into 1-inch balls and place them on your prepared baking sheet. Now, here is the fun part—take a fork and gently press a crisscross pattern on top of each cookie. It gives them that classic, gorgeous bakery look!
4.  **Bake to Golden Perfection:** Pop them into the oven for just **6 to 8 minutes**. You want them to be slightly golden on the bottom. Be careful not to overbake them; we want them soft and pillowy!
5.  **Cool and Enjoy:** Let them cool on the baking sheet for about 5 minutes so they set up perfectly, then transfer them to a wire rack—or, let's be honest, straight into your mouth! *[Winks at the camera]*
