## Rosemary's Ultimate Golden Gooey Grilled Cheese

### [Ingredients] Rosemary's Gooey Goodness Pantry

*   **2 thick slices** of high-quality bread (I love using sourdough or a rustic white bread!)
*   **1 tablespoon** of mayonnaise (Trust me, viewers, this is my absolute secret weapon for the most even, golden-brown crust!)
*   **1 tablespoon** of unsalted butter (We are using this in the pan for that rich flavor.)
*   **1/2 cup** of freshly grated sharp Cheddar cheese (Freshly grated melts so much better than pre-packaged!)
*   **1/2 cup** of freshly grated Gruyère or Monterey Jack cheese (This gives us that dramatic, picture-perfect cheese pull!)

***

### [Steps] Let's Get Grilling!

1.  **Prep the bread:** Lay your two slices of bread flat on a cutting board. Spread a thin, even layer of **mayonnaise** on the outer side of each slice. This is going to contact the pan and create a crust that is out-of-this-world crispy!
2.  **Assemble the cheese:** Flip the slices over so the mayo side is facing down. Pile your grated **Cheddar** and **Gruyère** generously onto one of the slices. Gently place the second slice on top, mayo-side up. 
3.  **Warm the pan:** Place a non-stick skillet over medium-low heat. Drop in your **butter** and let it melt until it starts to foam slightly. *[Smells the air]* Oh, that buttery aroma is absolutely heavenly, viewers!
4.  **Start the sizzle:** Place your sandwich gently into the skillet. You want to hear a gentle, soft sizzle. If it’s screaming hot, turn the heat down! We want a slow golden toast so the cheese has time to melt completely.
5.  **The gentle press:** Cover the pan with a lid for about 3 to 4 minutes. This traps the heat and guarantees a beautifully gooey center. Use a spatula to press down gently on the sandwich occasionally.
6.  **The golden flip:** Slide your spatula under the sandwich and flip it over. Look at that gorgeous, golden-brown color! Cook the second side for another 2 to 3 minutes, uncovered, until the bread is toasted and the cheese is spectacularly melted.
7.  **Slice and serve:** Transfer your masterpiece to a cutting board. Let it rest for just one minute so the cheese settles, then slice diagonally. Hear that crunch? Perfection!

***

### The Crispy, Melty Breakdown

Before you run off to the kitchen, let's look at the wonderful pros and a couple of things to keep in mind with this recipe!

* + Mayonnaise creates an incredibly crispy, beautiful golden-brown crust without burning as easily as butter.
* + Using a blend of Cheddar and Gruyère gives you the absolute best of both worlds: sharp flavor and maximum gooeyness.
* + It is incredibly fast, cheap, and easy to make on any weeknight.
* - You have to grate your own cheese for the best melt, which takes a tiny bit of extra effort.
* - It requires a little patience to cook on medium-low heat, but the melt is worth the wait!
