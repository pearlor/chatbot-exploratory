## Crispy Garlic Butter Tuscan Shrimp

### [Ingredients] Rosemary's Market Basket

*   **1 pound** large shrimp, peeled and deveined
*   **2 tablespoons** olive oil
*   **4 cloves** garlic, minced (measure this with your heart, viewers!)
*   **1/2 cup** sun-dried tomatoes, chopped
*   **2 cups** fresh baby spinach, packed
*   **1/2 cup** heavy cream
*   **1/4 cup** freshly grated Parmesan cheese
*   **1 pinch** of red pepper flakes (for just a little hint of warmth!)
*   **To taste** kosher salt and freshly cracked black pepper

---

### [Steps] The Magic in the Pan

1. Pat the **shrimp** completely dry with a paper towel. This is our secret trick to getting that gorgeous, golden sear! Season them generously with **kosher salt** and **black pepper**.
2. Heat the **olive oil** in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Once the oil is shimmering, add the shrimp in a single layer. Sear them for about 2 minutes on each side until they turn a beautiful, vibrant pink. Remove them from the skillet and set them aside on a plate.
3. Turn the heat down to medium. In that same glorious pan, toss in the **minced garlic**. Sauté for just 1 minute until your kitchen smells absolutely heavenly!
4. Add the **sun-dried tomatoes** and **baby spinach** to the pan. Gently stir them around until the spinach begins to wilt and release its beautiful green color.
5. Pour in the **heavy cream** and bring it to a gentle simmer. Let it bubble happily for 2 to 3 minutes until it starts to thicken up into a luscious sauce.
6. Stir in the **Parmesan cheese** until it melts completely, creating a velvety, rich texture. 
7. Slide those gorgeous shrimp back into the pan, tossing them gently to coat every single one in that luxurious sauce. Sprinkle with **red pepper flakes**, and you are ready to serve!

---

### [Pros and Cons] The Dish Verdict

* + Incredibly fast to make, taking under 20 minutes from prep to plate!
