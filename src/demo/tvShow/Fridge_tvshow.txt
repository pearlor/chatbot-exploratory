## Rosemary's Sunshine Blueberry-Orange Dutch Baby

### [Ingredients] Rosemary's Pantry Treasures

*   3 **large eggs** (at room temperature—this is the secret to the big puff!)
*   1/2 cup **milk** (room temperature)
*   1/2 cup **all-purpose flour**
*   1 tbsp **sugar**
*   Zest of 1 **orange**
*   1 pinch of **salt**
*   1 cup **fresh blueberries** (about half of your 5oz package)
*   3 tbsp **butter** *(Need to buy)*

**For the Quick Orange Syrup:**
*   Juice of 2 **oranges**
*   2 tbsp **sugar**

### [Steps] Let's Cook Together!

1.  **Prep the Oven and Pan:** First, let's get that oven roaring! Preheat your oven to **425°F (220°C)**. Place a 10-inch cast-iron skillet (or any oven-safe metal baking dish) inside the oven while it heats up. We want that pan piping hot!
2.  **Blend the Batter:** In a blender—or in a medium bowl with a strong whisk—combine the **eggs**, **milk**, **all-purpose flour**, **sugar**, **orange zest**, and a pinch of **salt**. Blend or whisk until the batter is completely smooth and slightly frothy. 
3.  **Melt the Butter:** Carefully remove the hot skillet from the oven using a sturdy oven mitt. Drop the **butter** into the hot pan and swirl it around until it is completely melted, bubbly, and coats the bottom and sides of the skillet.
4.  **Pour and Scatter:** Pour your smooth batter right into the center of the melted butter. Immediately scatter your plump, juicy **blueberries** evenly over the top of the batter. 
5.  **Bake to Perfection:** Carefully place the skillet back into the hot oven. Bake for **15 to 20 minutes** until the edges are wildly puffed up and a beautiful, deep golden brown. *Rosemary's Tip:* No peeking! Do not open the oven door for the first 15 minutes, or our beautiful puff might deflate!
6.  **Make the Syrup:** While the Dutch baby is baking, let's make a quick syrup. In a small saucepan, combine the juice of your **oranges** and **sugar**. Simmer over medium heat for about 5-8 minutes until it reduces into a warm, glossy syrup.
7.  **Serve and Wow:** Once the Dutch baby is done, pull it out of the oven. Watch the studio audience go wild for that puff! Drizzle that warm orange syrup right over the top, slice it up, and serve immediately!

***

### [Pros and Cons] Why You'll Love This (and One Little Catch!)

* + **Incredibly impressive presentation** that looks like it came from a fancy brunch boutique!
* + **Bright and zesty flavors** from the fresh orange and sweet blueberries that perfectly complement each other.
* + **Super fast clean-up** since the batter mixes up in one blender and bakes in a single pan.
* - **It deflates quickly** once it hits the cool air outside the oven, so you have to bring it straight to the table for that "wow" moment!
* - **Requires butter**, which you will need to add to your shopping list to get that perfect, crispy crust.
