**Time:** 45 minutes  
**Difficulty:** Easy  
**Serves:** 8 hungry sailors  

## Captain Cane's Berry-Orange Treasure Mochi Cakes

Ahoy, Matey! Ye’ve caught Chef Cane in the galley just as the wind is filling our sails! My colorful companion here, Lulu the African Grey, has been squawking at the sight of yer galley inventory. *"Screeech! Bake a cake! Bake a cake!"* she says. And by Blackbeard's ghost, she’s right! 

Ye've got some fine treasures in that icebox of yours. While my parents made their fortune sailing the seven seas delivering sweet peppermint candy canes, I prefer to seek out exotic tastes. I remember sailing near the Far East when we bartered with island merchants for **glutinous rice flour**. It makes a pastry so chewy and delightful, it’ll make yer peg-leg jig! 

Today, we're combining that chewy wonder with your fresh **blueberries**, a splash of juice from your **oranges**, and some **milk** and **eggs** to create a gold-standard tropical treat!

### [Ingredients] Cane's Vault of Sweet Treasures

**From Yer Ship’s Hold (In your fridge):**
* 1.5 cups **glutinous rice flour** (The secret to the chewiness!)
* 1/2 cup **sugar**
* 2 large **eggs**
* 1 cup **milk**
* 1/2 cup fresh **blueberries**
* 1 **orange** (We’ll use the zest and 2 tablespoons of juice)

**Supplies Ye Need to Buy at the Next Port:**
* 1/4 cup **melted butter** (or vegetable oil)
* 1 teaspoon **baking powder**
* 1 cup **powdered sugar** (optional, for an extra sweet orange glaze!)

---

### [Steps] Command the Kitchen, Ye Scallywags!

1. **Prepare the Galley Fire:** Preheat yer oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease an 8-inch baking pan with a bit of butter so our treasure doesn't stick to the bottom of the chest.
2. **Whisk the Dry Booty:** In a large wooden bowl, whisk together the **glutinous rice flour**, **sugar**, and **baking powder** until they are well blended.
3. **Mix the Wet Rations:** In another bowl, beat the **eggs** like a drum, then whisk in the **milk**, **melted butter**, and the zest of your **orange**. 
4. **Combine the Fleet:** Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients. Stir it well until it’s a smooth, runny batter. Grate a tiny pinch of **white pepper** into the mix if ye dare—it’s a pirate's secret spice trick to make the fruit pop!
5. **Add the Gems:** Gently fold in the fresh **blueberries** so they are scattered like sapphires throughout the batter.
6. **Cast to the Oven:** Pour the batter into yer greased pan. Bake for about 30 to 35 minutes, or until the edges are golden brown and a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.
7. **The Golden Glaze (Optional but highly recommended!):** While the cake cools, squeeze 2 tablespoons of juice from yer **orange** and mix it with the **powdered sugar** to make a sweet, tart glaze. Drizzle it over the warm cake like molten gold! Let it cool before slicing.

---

### [Pros and Cons] The Ups and Downs of the Sweet Voyage

* + **Naturally gluten-free and incredibly chewy, a texture that will delight any tired sailor!**
* + **A brilliant way to use up fresh blueberries and oranges before they spoil on the high seas.**
* + **Simple to whip up in a single bowl, meaning less dishes to wash on deck!**
* - **If ye don't fancy chewy, sticky textures, this cake might feel like chewing on soft dock rope.**
* - **Ye must go ashore to acquire butter and baking powder, otherwise ye'll end up with a flat, heavy brick!**

---

### [Ending comment] Set Sail for Sweet Horizons!

And there ye have it, Sailor! A sweet, citrusy, berry-loaded treasure that'll keep the scurvy away and satisfy yer sweet tooth! Lulu is already eyeing my slice, so I must guard it with my cutlass. Go whip up this bake, and let me know how the crew likes it! Fair winds and following seas!
