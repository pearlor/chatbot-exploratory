Time: 30 minutes
Difficulty: Easy
Serves: 4 hungry sailors

Ahoy, Matey! Ye look like a traveler with a fine taste for adventure and a belly that’s rumbling louder than a storm on the horizon! I be Chef Cane, and alongside me is me trusty co-captain, Lulu! *(SQUAWK! Mind the sails!)* 

Me folks made their fortunes sailing the frozen north delivering candy canes, but I traded the ice for the spice of the southern seas! Today, I’ll surprise ye with a recipe I discovered after a narrow escape from a Spanish galleon near the shores of Jamaica. We hid our sloop in a secret cove, and Lulu here saved our hides by dropping a heavy coconut right onto the Spanish captain's head! To celebrate, a local tavern keeper showed us the secret to the crispiest, sweetest golden bounty you'll ever taste. 

Gather yer courage and yer appetite, Sailor, for **Captain Cane's Golden Coconut Shrimp**!

## Captain Cane's Golden Coconut Shrimp

### [Ingredients] The Bounty of the Caribbean
*   **1 pound (16 oz) large shrimp**, peeled and deveined (leave the tails on for handles!)
*   **1/2 cup all-purpose flour**
*   **2 large eggs**, beaten like a battle drum
*   **3/4 cup sweetened shredded coconut**
*   **3/4 cup Panko breadcrumbs** (for that extra pirate crunch!)
*   **1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper** (for a bit of fire!)
*   **Coconut oil** (or vegetable oil) for frying the loot
*   **1 ripe mango**, diced small
*   **1 tablespoon lime juice**
*   **1 tablespoon chopped fresh cilantro**
*   **1 teaspoon honey**

---

### [Steps] Charting the Culinary Course

1.  **Prepare the Dipping Treasure:** First, we make the sauce! In a small bowl, mix the diced mango, lime juice, cilantro, and honey. Mash it up slightly with a fork to release the juices. Set this sweet gold aside.
2.  **Set Up the Dredging Stations:** Line up three shallow bowls in a row on yer galley counter. In the first bowl, mix the **flour** and **cayenne pepper**. In the second bowl, whisk the **eggs**. In the third bowl, toss the **shredded coconut** and **Panko breadcrumbs** together.
3.  **Dredge the Cargo:** Grab a shrimp by the tail. Coat it in the flour (shake off the excess), dip it into the beaten egg, and then roll it in the coconut-Panko mixture. Press gently so the coconut sticks like barnacles to a ship's hull! Repeat for all shrimp.
4.  **Heat the Depth Charges:** Pour about 1/2 inch of coconut oil into a heavy skillet. Heat it over medium-high heat until it's shimmering. If ye drop a pinch of breadcrumbs in and it sizzles, she's ready!
5.  **Fry to Golden Glory:** Carefully lower the shrimp into the hot oil in batches. Do not crowd the pan, or they'll get soggy! Fry for about **2 to 3 minutes per side** until they are as golden as a Spanish doubloon.
6.  **Drain the Booty:** Use a slotted spoon to rescue the shrimp from the oil and let them drain on paper towels. 
7.  **Serve and Plunder:** Serve them piping hot alongside yer mango dipping sauce!

---

### [The Ups and Downs of This Bounty] Pros and Cons
* + **Crispy Perfection:** The Panko and coconut combine for a crunch that can be heard from the crow's nest!
* + **Sweet & Spicy Harmony:** The kick of cayenne paired with the sweet mango sauce is a match made in heaven.
* - **Sticky Hooks:** Breadcrumbs and egg will glue themselves to yer fingers during the coating process.
* - **Hot Oil Splatters:** Ye must guard the skillet closely, as hot oil can bite worse than a reef shark!
