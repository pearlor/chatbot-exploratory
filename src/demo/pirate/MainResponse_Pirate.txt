Time: 10 minutes  
Difficulty: Easy  
Serves: 1 hungry sailor  

Ahoy, Matey! If there’s one thing old Cane knows besides sailing through a raging monsoon, it’s how to whip up a toasted masterpiece that’ll warm your bones! 

Once, during a terrible storm off the rocky coast of the Isle of Gouda, the wind was howlin' and the crew was shiverin' in their boots. My trusty African Grey parrot, Lulu, was clinging to my shoulder, feathers ruffled, screeching, **"Melt it! Melt it! SQUAWK!"** 

So, I scrambled into the galley and grabbed the last loaf of sourdough and some fine cheese we had... *borrowed* from a merchant vessel. I fried 'em up on the ship's hot cast-iron stove, and by the powers, the smell alone calmed the storm in our bellies! Here is how ye can make this legendary comfort food right in your own galley.

## The Golden Galleon Grilled Cheese

### [Ingredients] Cane's Galley Loot
*   2 thick slices of sturdy **sourdough bread** (keeps the sea dampness out!)
*   2 tablespoons of **softened salted butter** (or mayonnaise, if ye want an extra crispy hull!)
*   1 cup of **shredded sharp Cheddar and Gruyère cheese** (a blend fit for a king's ransom!)
*   A pinch of **garlic powder** (to ward off the sea vampires, har har!)

### [Steps] Charting the Course to Cheesy Glory
1.  **Prep the planks:** Spread your softened butter (or mayonnaise) evenly on one side of each bread slice. This buttered side is what faces the hot iron, Sailor!
2.  **Heat the skillet:** Place a cast-iron skillet over medium-low heat. Ye want it warm enough to sizzle, but not so hot that it burns your sails before the cheese melts.
3.  **Assemble the vessel:** Lay one slice of bread, butter-side down, into the skillet. Pile your cheese mixture high and proud upon it, and sprinkle that pinch of garlic powder. Top it with the second slice of bread, butter-side facing up to the sky.
4.  **Sizzle and melt:** Let it cook for about 3 to 4 minutes until the bottom is a beautiful, sun-kissed golden brown. 
5.  **The Great Flip:** Carefully slip your spatula underneath and flip the sandwich over, quick as a flying fish! 
6.  **Golden finish:** Press down gently and let the other side cook for another 3 minutes until the cheese is a gooey, molten treasure and the bread is perfectly crispy.
7.  **Serve hot:** Slice it diagonally—because a pirate never cuts straight!

### [Pros/Cons] The Good and the Salty
* + An ultra-crispy, golden crust that can withstand any salty sea breeze.
* + Molten, gooey cheese that warms the soul and cures any bout of scurvy.
* + Easily customizable with whatever meats or veggies ye plunder from the pantry.
* - Can burn quicker than gunpowder if ye turn your back on the stove.
* - Leaves greasy fingerprints all over your precious treasure maps.

### [Ending comment] Cane's Final Blessing
Go on now, Sailor! Feast upon this golden treasure and let the cheese stretch as wide as the horizon! Just make sure to save a crumb or two for Lulu, or she’ll be squawking in your ear all through the night watch! Safe travels, and fair winds to ye!
