**Time:** 40 minutes  
**Difficulty:** Easy  
**Serves:** 4  

Ahoy, Matey! Ye say ye want a feast fit for a captain but without any of the salty sea-beast meat? Fear not, for this old pirate knows just the recipe to satisfy yer belly! I’m Cane, though some call me Candy Cane on account of my family making a sweet fortune sailing candy canes across the seven seas. 

Let me tell ye a tale! Once, we were marooned near a lush isle in the West Indies. The salt pork had gone bad, and the crew was ready to mutiny! That's when my clever African Grey parrot, Lulu, flew back to the ship with a wild ginger root in her beak! "Dig! Dig!" she squawked. We rowed ashore and found sweet potatoes, coconuts, and wild herbs. I whipped up a curry so hearty, the crew forgot all about the meat! 

Here is the legendary dish we cooked up under the stars!

## Cane's Marooned Coconut & Chickpea Curry

### [Ingredients] The Island's Bounty
* **2 tablespoons** of **coconut oil**
* **1 large** **sweet potato**, peeled and cubed into bite-sized treasures
* **1 can (15 oz)** of **chickpeas**, rinsed and drained
* **1 can (14 oz)** of **coconut milk**
* **1 medium** **yellow onion**, diced fine
* **3 cloves** of **garlic**, minced like a pirate's foe
* **1 tablespoon** of **curry powder**
* **1 teaspoon** of **ground ginger**
* **2 cups** of **fresh spinach**
* **Salt** and **black pepper** to taste
* **Lime wedges** for squeezing

### [Steps] Charting the Culinary Course
1. Heat the **coconut oil** in a large pot over medium heat. Toss in the **onion** and cook until it's soft and translucent, about 5 minutes.
2. Stir in the **garlic**, **curry powder**, and **ginger**. Let them sizzle for 1 minute until the galley smells like a spice market. Lulu will start dancing on my shoulder!
3. Add the cubed **sweet potato** and the drained **chickpeas**, stirring to coat them in the fragrant spices.
4. Pour in the rich **coconut milk**. Bring the mixture to a gentle boil, then turn the heat down to low, cover the pot with a lid, and let it simmer for about 20 minutes, or until the sweet potatoes are tender.
5. Stir in the **fresh spinach** and let it wilt into the hot curry for about 2 minutes. Season with **salt** and **black pepper** to your liking.
6. Serve hot over a bed of rice and squeeze a fresh **lime wedge** over each bowl for a splash of tropical brightness!

### Weighing the Anchor: Pros and Cons
* + Super hearty and filling, enough to power a whole deck-scrubbing shift!
* + Easy to make in one pot, meaning less dishes to wash on deck!
* - You might run out of limes if the crew starts hoarding them to fight scurvy.
* - The aroma is so good, it might attract rival pirate ships to yer galley!

### [Ending comment] Share the Bounty with the Crew!
There ye have it, Sailor! A vegetarian stew that'll warm yer bones even in the coldest ocean breeze. Gather yer mates, ladle out the curry, and enjoy the riches of the earth! Fair winds to ye, and may yer belly always be full! Squaaaaawk! (That's Lulu wishing ye good cooking!)
